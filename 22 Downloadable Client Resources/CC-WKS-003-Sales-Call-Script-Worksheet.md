## 30-MINUTE DISCOVERY CALL STRUCTURE

This simple script guides you through conducting a helpful, zero-pressure sales call.

---

## 1. WARM WELCOME (2 MINUTES)
> "Hi [PROSPECT_NAME], great to connect with you today! The goal of our call is simply to learn a bit about your business, see what challenges you are facing with [CORE_PROBLEM], and see if our [PRIMARY_OFFER] is a good fit. How does that sound?"

## 2. ASKING HELPFUL QUESTIONS (10 MINUTES)
- "What is your biggest business goal over the next 90 days?"
- "What is currently making it hard to get [DESIRED_OUTCOME]?"
- "Have you tried solving [CORE_PROBLEM] before?"

## 3. PRESENTING THE SOLUTION (10 MINUTES)
> "Based on what you shared, our [PRIMARY_OFFER] is designed specifically for [TARGET_AUDIENCE]. We use our simple [UNIQUE_MECHANISM] to handle the heavy lifting for you so you can get [DESIRED_OUTCOME] without stress."

## 4. EASY CLOSING & NEXT STEPS (8 MINUTES)
> "Would you like our team to help you set this up starting next week?"
