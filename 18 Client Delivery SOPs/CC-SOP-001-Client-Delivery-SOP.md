2. **Step 2 - Strategy & Positioning:** Finalize brand messaging (`CC-BRD-001`) and offer strategy (`CC-STR-001`).

5. **Step 5 - QA & Launch:** Execute pre-launch quality-control checklist.

## 3. DEFINITION OF DONE & QUALITY CONTROL
- All PHP code passes `php -l` lint without errors.
- Forms submit cleanly with nonce validation.
- Mobile Core Web Vitals score > 90.
- Client signs off on handoff documentation (`CC-TRN-001`).
